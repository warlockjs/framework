# Contact form

A visitor sends a message to the team from the home page.

## Why

Visitors need one obvious way to reach the team, and the form has to keep what they typed when something goes wrong.

## Flows

- send-message a visitor fills in the form and sends a message

## Affects and affected by

- affected by design-system/direction (the form follows the reading direction)
- affected by design-system/components (the form controls)
- affected by design-system/error-messages (what each error says)
